Pattern check

A blank board for the four or five squares you are actually staring at. Copy in their pencil marks, say which technique you think you are looking at, and this reads the conditions of that pattern back to you one at a time — ticked where your marks settle them, and named as yours to check where they cannot. It solves nothing, and it never guesses: an empty square here means you have not told me, not anything is possible.

Pencil marks

Tap a square, then press digits to write its pencil marks

What do you think it is?

Mark the squares that make the pattern, and nothing else. To let it check a count as well — that a line really has only two spots — mark the rest of that line too, then select just the pattern squares before you press.

What this can and cannot know

Every technique splits into conditions about the squares in front of you and conditions about the squares you have not written down.

  • ✓Settled by your marks. A naked pair and an XY-Wing are nothing but conditions of this kind, so a yes on those is a real yes.
  • ?A claim about squares you have not typed — “row 2 holds no other 6”. This cannot see them, so it names the scan instead of assuming it. Type more of that line in and it resolves, one way or the other.
  • ✗Contradicted, either by where the squares sit or by a mark you typed. This is the answer you came for.

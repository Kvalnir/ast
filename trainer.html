Pattern trainer

Autofill, autocheck and notes cleared on entry, the way News+ has them, so the reflexes carry over. The controls go further on purpose: a pad for writing digits and a pad for marking notes, candidates you cross off rather than delete, and a coach that reads your position and names the techniques available in it — at whatever level of help you ask for.

—

Stuck on a puzzle somewhere else — News+, a paper, an app? Type in the digits it prints and the coach reads that one. It works out the solution and which techniques the puzzle needs; you only enter the givens.

Drill one technique

Jumps to a real position where that technique is the move to find. Everything simpler has already been played.

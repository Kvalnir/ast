Pattern trainer

The board behaves like News+ — Pen and Notes, autofill, autocheck, notes cleared on entry. The difference is that it watches your position and tells you which techniques are available, at whatever level of help you ask for.

Import a puzzle

—

Stuck on a puzzle somewhere else — News+, a paper, an app? Type in the digits it prints and the coach reads that one. It works out the solution and which techniques the puzzle needs; you only enter the givens.

Experimental. Reading digits off a picture is guesswork in a way that typing them is not, so this one lands its answer on the board for you to check rather than importing it. Squares it is unsure of are marked. A screenshot works far better than a photograph of a screen, and nothing leaves this device.

Drill one technique

Jumps to a real position where that technique is the move to find. Everything simpler has already been played.
